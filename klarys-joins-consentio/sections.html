What is Klarys?

Founded in 2016 in Lausanne and Rennes, Klarys develops software for procurement, demand forecasting and supplier collaboration across fresh produce supply chains.

The platform helps retailers, wholesalers and food manufacturers optimise sourcing, structure their procurement processes and improve operational performance, particularly in the seafood, meat and dairy categories.

Klarys counts several major European retail chains and food groups among its customers.
