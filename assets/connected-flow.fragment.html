The connected workflow

One workflow, from demand plan to synchronized order data.

Each step feeds the next. Buyers and suppliers work on the same structured information instead of exchanging files, emails and messages.

01

Forecast

Build demand plans by category, store group and period.

Without Consentio
  • Scattered historical data
  • Manual spreadsheet calculations
With Consentio
  • Centralized sales and historical data
  • AI-powered demand forecasting
  • Granular visibility by store, category and period
02

Collect

Collect prices, volumes and availability in one comparable format.

Without Consentio
  • Offers through email, Excel and messages
  • Manual consolidation by buyers
With Consentio
  • Structured supplier requests
  • Comparable prices, volumes and availability
  • Real-time visibility for buyers
03

Allocation

Distribute volumes across suppliers with full decision context.

Without Consentio
  • Decisions based on fragmented information
  • Manual supplier comparisons
With Consentio
  • All offers compared in one place
  • Faster volume allocation
  • Transparent and traceable decisions
04

Order execution

Track confirmations, amendments, deliveries and documents.

Without Consentio
  • Orders created manually from allocations
  • Changes exchanged by email or phone
With Consentio
  • Allocations converted into orders
  • Centralized confirmations and amendments
  • Validated order data synced with ERP
05

Modular approach

Start with a focused use case, prove value quickly, then expand progressively across categories, workflows and teams.

Without Consentio
  • Big-bang rollouts that take months
  • Value proven only after full deployment
With Consentio
  • Focused pilot live in weeks
  • Measurable impact before extending scope
  • Progressive rollout across categories and teams
