Why fresh is different

Fresh categories cannot be managed like standard goods.

Prices, availability and quality change daily. Purchasing decisions are taken with incomplete information and executed across disconnected channels.

Stable · Predictable · Structured

Standard grocery purchasing

  • Stable references and prices
  • Long negotiation cycles
  • Predictable lead times
  • Contracted volumes

Standard procurement tools are designed for this reality.
